# Business & Startups: From Zero to Building Your Own Company
### For anyone who has ever wondered how the world of business actually works

> **Who is this for?** Anyone. You do not need an MBA. You do not need to have started a company before. You do not need to understand finance or economics. If you are a student who has heard the word "startup" and wants to know what it actually means — this is for you. If you are a working professional who wants to start something of your own but has no idea where to begin — this is for you too. If you are a developer, a designer, an engineer, or a creative who wants to understand the business world they operate inside — this is for you.
>
> By the end of this course, you will understand how every major kind of business works, how money flows, how strategies are built, how startups get funded, and what it actually takes to build a company from scratch. You will have studied the world's most important companies in depth. And most importantly, you will know enough to take your first real steps toward building something of your own.

---

## What You Will Learn

- **How businesses are built** — the fundamentals of finance, marketing, sales, operations, and law
- **How to think strategically** — competitive moats, blue oceans, pricing psychology, and brand building
- **How startups work** — from finding an idea to raising money to achieving product-market fit
- **How funding works** — angels, VCs, term sheets, valuations, IPOs, and exits explained plainly
- **The major business models** — SaaS, marketplace, platform, subscription, freemium, and more
- **The world's most important companies** — deep case studies of Apple, Amazon, Google, Airbnb, Stripe, and more
- **The new economy** — creator economy, Web3, gig platforms, AI-first companies, and climate tech
- **What it takes to lead** — founder psychology, managing failure, building culture, and knowing what you actually want

## How to Use This Course

### If you have never thought about business before:
Start at Chapter 00. Every chapter assumes you know nothing going in. Read in order through Part 1 before jumping anywhere else.

### If you know business basics but want to understand startups:
Skim Part 0, read Part 1 for gaps, then start carefully from Part 4 (Chapter 23).

### If you are already working at a startup and want to go deeper:
Jump directly to the parts most relevant to your role. Use the case studies in Part 8 as ongoing reference. Come back to Part 2 for strategic frameworks.

### If you want to raise money:
Read Parts 4 and 5 first to make sure you are building the right foundation, then work through Part 6 (Chapter 35-41) carefully.

### If you just want to understand the world better:
Read the case studies in Part 8 and the business model breakdowns in Part 7. They work as standalone reads.
