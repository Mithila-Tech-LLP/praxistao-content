# Physics: From Zero to Understanding the Universe

## A Complete Beginner's Course — For Anyone Who Wants to Understand How the Physical World Works

> **"Physics is the study of everything — from the smallest particle to the largest galaxy. It is the language the universe speaks."**

---

## Who This Course Is For

- Students starting physics for the first time
- Anyone who wonders *why* the sky is blue, *how* a plane flies, *what* electricity actually is
- People who want to understand the science behind everyday things
- Curious minds of any age — no prior physics knowledge required

**No advanced math required. Formulas are explained step by step.**

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this course you will understand:

- **Mechanics** — why things move, fall, spin, and collide
- **Thermodynamics** — heat, engines, entropy, and why ice melts
- **Electricity** — how electrons flow and power your devices
- **Magnetism** — magnets, motors, generators, and transformers
- **Optics** — light, lenses, mirrors, and rainbows
- **Waves** — sound, vibration, and the electromagnetic spectrum
- **Modern Physics** — relativity, quantum mechanics, atoms, and nuclear energy
- **Electronics** — how semiconductors and transistors work
