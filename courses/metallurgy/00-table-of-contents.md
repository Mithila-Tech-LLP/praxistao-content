# Metallurgy — A Complete Learning Guide
### From Atoms and Crystal Structures to Single-Crystal Jet Engine Turbine Blades

> **"Metallurgy is the art and science of extracting metals from their ores, purifying them, and creating useful alloys. It is the reason your airplane flies, your bridge stands, and your phone exists."**

---

## What You Will Learn

This course takes you on a complete journey through the science of metals — starting from the atom itself, building up through crystal structure, defects, phase diagrams, mechanical properties, and heat treatment, through the major families of engineering metals, all the way to the most advanced engineering material ever created: the single-crystal nickel superalloy turbine blade that enables modern jet engines to operate above the melting point of most metals.

No prior metallurgy knowledge required. High-school chemistry and physics are enough to start.

---

```
Atoms → Crystals → Defects → Phase Diagrams → Mechanical Properties

```

## Prerequisites

- High-school chemistry (atomic structure, periodic table, basic bonding)
- High-school physics (forces, stress = force/area, energy concepts)
- No prior metallurgy needed — this course builds everything from scratch

*This course was designed to take you from "what is an atom?" all the way to understanding why a jet engine can operate at temperatures that would melt most metals — and why that required inventing an entirely new class of materials over 70 years of research.*
