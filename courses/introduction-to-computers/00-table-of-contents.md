# Introduction to Computers: From Zero to Understanding Everything

## A Complete Beginner's Guide — For Anyone Who Wants to Understand Computers

> **If you have never touched a computer before, or if you have used one but never understood what's really happening inside — this course is for you. No experience needed. No jargon. Just clear, simple explanations of everything.**

---

## Who This Course Is For

- A child seeing a computer for the first time
- A parent who wants to understand what their kids are using
- A student who wants to understand technology before diving into coding
- Anyone who uses a phone or laptop but wonders *how any of this actually works*

**No math. No coding. No previous knowledge required.**

---

## What You Will Learn

By the end of this course you will understand:

- What a computer actually **is** — and how it thinks
- The **history** of computers — from room-sized machines to your pocket
- **Hardware** — the physical parts you can touch (CPU, RAM, keyboard, screen)
- **Software** — the invisible programs that make it all work
- **Operating systems** — Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS
- **The Internet** — how billions of computers talk to each other
- **The Web** — websites, browsers, and search engines
- **Programming** — how people write instructions computers can follow
- **Artificial Intelligence** — how computers learn and make decisions
- **The Cloud** — what it means to store things "online"
