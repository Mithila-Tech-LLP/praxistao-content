## Overview

Backend engineering is the craft of building the systems other software depends on: the APIs mobile and web apps call, the databases that hold everyone's data, and the services that keep working at 3am when nobody is watching. This roadmap is a sequenced path through that craft, using Go as the language throughout because its simplicity, built-in concurrency, and dominance in real backend systems make it one of the most direct routes from "I can write a function" to "I can ship a production service."

Each node below points at real chapters from the site's own courses and real hands-on projects — not just a list of buzzwords. Work through the sections roughly in order; a few nodes are marked **optional**, meaning they deepen your understanding but aren't required to move on. Nodes with a practice project attached are where the reading turns into something you built and tested yourself.
