## Overview

Blockchains sit at the intersection of cryptography, distributed systems, and plain old backend engineering — a block is really just a struct, a P2P network is really just a bunch of TCP connections, and a smart contract is really just a tiny virtual machine. This roadmap walks through building all of it yourself, in Go, rather than treating blockchain as a black box.

It follows the exact structure of the site's own 95-chapter Blockchain in Go course, grouped into a sequence you can actually work through: cryptography first, then blocks and consensus, then transactions and wallets, then networking, then storage and smart contracts. Nodes marked **optional** go deeper into topics (alternate consensus algorithms, real-world chain architecture) that aren't required to finish the core path but are worth returning to once you have.
